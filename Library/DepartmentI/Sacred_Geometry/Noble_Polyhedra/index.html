NOBLE POLYHEDRA

Polyhedral Symmetry & Self-Intersecting Faces

The Second Lesson · Beyond the Five

Relax one law. Find a whole new kingdom.

Lesson 1 proved there are exactly five Platonic solids. This lesson asks what happens when we loosen the rules: let faces pass through one another, let them become irregular — but keep every face and every vertex identical under symmetry. The result is the noble polyhedra: two infinite families and, as of the 2020s, a closed census of 146 sporadic forms.

Five sections, each with five interactive 3D steps — from the Kepler-Poinsot star solids through Hess's paper models and Webb's software discoveries to Connor Hill's complete classification. Finish all five sections to earn the lesson's single Tome.
