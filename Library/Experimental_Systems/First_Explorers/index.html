    What is real here, and what is a draft

    This page is a first-principles introduction. It runs entirely in your browser: nothing is uploaded, stored or published, and no account is needed.

    Real, and shared with the rest of Owl Academy

    • The packets and chains. Every chain here is grown by growBiochain / extendBiochain in scripts/biomesh-core.js, unchanged: the shdccp/2 stream of RFC 07, its Merkle root, weaves and engram layer.
    • The strict check. The first six Overwatch checks are verifyIntegrityStrict (RFC 07 section 12), check for check.
    • The signatures. ECDSA P-256 through Web Crypto, the same primitive as the Tome Seals (scripts/seal-crypto.js).

    New in this page: draft 0, not yet specified in an RFC

    • OBS/1 (an observation as one canonical line), LEXICON/1 (the exact address sense.index of each observation), TRACE/1 (the hash-chained ledger), OVERWATCH/1 (the certificate line) and HYPERPACK/1 (the bundle a receiver checks). They live in scripts/strix-overwatch.js with unit tests.
    • The 2D disc and 3D ball positions are computed from the exact address for display. They are floating-point and are never signed or committed; only the address is.

    Limits to keep in mind

    • The signing key is made for this visit and then forgotten. A valid signature shows the certificate has not changed since this page issued it. It does not show who issued it: binding Overwatch to a registered Genesis seal is later work.
    • STRIX here is a scripted guide. The engram familiar lives in Engram Mind Eye; this page does not run it.
    • The lexicon here is the small one you build. The Hyperbolic Lexicon's exact addresses and its attested package verifier are not connected yet.
    • The receiver is simulated in this same page: the package is turned into text, optionally damaged, and verified again from that text alone.
