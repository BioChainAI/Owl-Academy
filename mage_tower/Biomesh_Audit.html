The strict audit is an Archon-only review surface. Your Genesis tier is being verified against the registrar.

What this page does

It reads every record in biochains, page by page in document-id order, and runs verifyIntegrityStrict (RFC 07 section 12) on each: parity, canonical, crystal, leaves, weaves, derived, stopping at the first failing check. It writes nothing. The report stays in this browser until you download it.

Labels (decision D15): strict ok; legacy (no shdccp/2 stream or streamVersion); failed at <check>; self-consistent only when the older verifyIntegrity still passes. Weave mismatch counts the records that fail at weaves because weaveChiral or weaveMirror does not reproduce: they may have been grown in a browser whose Math.hypot differs from V8's (RFC 07 section 8.7), so they are counted on their own.

The weaves are recomputed with this browser's arithmetic. Before it reads anything, the audit compares this browser's Math.hypot and a seeded weave with V8's values; when they differ, the page says so and the report records it (auditEngineMatchesV8: false), because weave mismatches may then come from this browser. Until the engine measurement (decision D7) is done, run the audit in a Chromium-based browser.
